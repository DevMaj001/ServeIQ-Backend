# ServeIQ — Business Owner Onboarding Guide

> Goal: from zero to taking your first real order in **under 15 minutes**.

---

## Before You Start

Have these ready:

- Your business name, type (bar / lounge / restaurant / club), and address
- Owner email + phone (you'll log in with these)
- A password (min 8 characters)
- Rough count of tables
- Your menu (names + prices) — CSV upload supported if you have it in a spreadsheet

---

## Step 1 — Register Your Business (2 min)

1. Open the Admin dashboard → **Register**.
2. Enter business name, type, owner name, email, phone, password.
3. Submit. This automatically creates:
   - Your business
   - A default **Main Branch**
   - Your **Owner** account

You are now logged in.

## Step 2 — Add Your Tables (3 min)

1. Go to **Tables → Create Table**.
2. For each table enter: table number (e.g. T1), label (optional), capacity.
3. Repeat for every table. You can add more later.

## Step 3 — Build Your Menu (5 min)

1. Go to **Menu Items → Create**.
2. For each item: name, category (Drinks, Food, Spirits…), price in ₦, unit (bottle, glass, plate).
3. Many items? Use **CSV Import**: columns = name, category, price, unit. Prices you type are in Naira; ServeIQ stores exact kobo internally so bills never drift.
4. Toggle availability off for anything sold out today.

## Step 4 — Set Up Staff (3 min)

1. Go to **Staff → Invite/Add staff**.
2. Create each person with their role:

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Manager | Everything except owner settings; approve orders, void tabs |
| Supervisor | Approve orders, deliver, manage floor |
| Waiter | Open tabs, take orders, generate bills |
| Cashier | Record payments, close bills |

3. Waiters/supervisors get an auto-generated **4-digit PIN** — share it privately; they log in on the mobile app with PIN only.

## Step 5 — Money Settings (1 min)

Under **Business Settings** set:

- **Service charge %** (e.g. 10 — applied on billed totals)
- **Tax rate %** (e.g. 7.5 VAT)
- Leave at 0 if you don't charge them.

## Step 6 — First Test Order (2 min)

1. On the dashboard open a **Shift** (records your opening float).
2. Tap an available table → **Open Tab**.
3. Add 2–3 menu items. Watch the running bill update live.
4. **Generate Bill** → confirm total matches expectations (subtotal + service + tax).
5. **Pay** with cash → receipt appears. Done — that's the whole flow your waiters will use all night.

---

## Daily Rhythm

- **Opening:** owner/manager opens the shift with starting cash.
- **Closing:** close the shift — ServeIQ shows expected vs counted cash per method (cash / transfer / POS).
- **Weekly:** check Reports → sales, top items, and waiter performance.

## During Beta

Found something broken or confusing? Use the in-app feedback button or message the team directly — beta businesses get same-day response on billing-critical issues.
