## 2. The ServeIQ Platform

ServeIQ is a **hospitality operations platform** that provides digital tools for restaurants, bars, lounges, clubs, and hotels, including:

- **Digital Ordering:** Enabling waiters and customers to place and track orders.
- **Billing and Receipt Management:** Automatic bill calculation, split billing, and receipt generation (screen, PDF, thermal print).
- **Payment Recording:** Recording how customers paid (cash, bank transfer, POS, card) for reconciliation purposes.
- **Business Management:** Table management, menu management, staff management, department management, and multi-branch management.
- **Analytics and Reporting:** Sales dashboards, revenue tracking, peak-hour analysis, and shift closing reports.
- **Inventory Management:** Stock tracking, low-stock alerts, and reconciliation.
- **AI-Powered Features:** AI-generated business logic, insights, restock suggestions (subject to feature availability and version).
- **Order Tracking:** Public order tracking via tracking codes.

ServeIQ does **not** act as a payment processor in its core version (V1). Payment recording is for reconciliation only. Payment gateway integration (via Paystack) is introduced in later versions (V2+).
