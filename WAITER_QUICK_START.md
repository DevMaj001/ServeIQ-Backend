# ServeIQ — Waiter Quick-Start Card

> Print this. Everything you need fits on one page.

---

## 1. Log In

Open the ServeIQ app → enter your **4-digit PIN**.
Your name and branch show on top. That's it — no email or password needed.

## 2. The Table Grid

| Color | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟩 Green | Free — seat guests, tap to open a tab |
| 🟥 Red | Occupied — tap to see their running tab |
| ⬛ Grey/Inactive | Out of service — don't use |

Occupied tables show the customer name and running total.

## 3. Taking an Order

1. Tap your table's tab → **Add Items**.
2. Browse or search the menu → tap items, set quantity.
3. **Confirm** → order goes to the kitchen/bar.
4. Guests ordering again later? Just **Add Items** again — each batch is a new "round" on the same bill.

## 4. The Running Bill

The tab screen always shows every round, item by item, with the live total.
Show it to guests any time they ask — no arithmetic, no disputes.

## 5. Closing Out

1. When guests are done: **Generate Bill** (locks the tab from new items).
2. Collect payment → **Pay**: choose Cash / Transfer / POS / Card.
   - Transfer or POS: type the reference number from the alert/receipt.
3. The receipt appears instantly — show it, share it, or print it.

## 6. Useful Extras

- **Transfer Tab:** guests moving tables? Move the tab — history follows.
- **Cancel item:** only before it's delivered; a reason is recorded.
- **Offline?** Orders save on your phone and sync when signal returns — keep working.

## Golden Rules

1. One tab per table — always open it before the first order.
2. Never close a tab without recording payment.
3. Your PIN is yours — never share it.
